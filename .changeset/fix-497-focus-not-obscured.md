`ObjectPage`: a control reached with Tab or Shift+Tab is never even partly under the sticky Anchor Bar, nor past the bottom edge of the scroller where the save bar starts (WCAG 2.4.12 Focus Not Obscured, Enhanced; #497). Every element of the scroller outside the Anchor Bar now has a scroll margin: the bar's height plus 0.5rem at the top, the virtual keyboard's height plus 0.5rem at the bottom. The browser's own focus scrolling and `scrollIntoView` honour it, so the focus indicator stays clear too. Firefox scrolls a focused control only when it is entirely out of view, so a small fallback covers it: after a Tab moves the focus into the scroller, one frame later and before anything is painted, the scroller scrolls by the least that clears the control. It does nothing when the browser already did it, and it never moves the content for a focus given by script (`preventScroll` is respected) or by a click. The Anchor Bar's `<nav>` carries `data-object-page-anchor-bar`. Sections still stop 4px below the bar, and the scroller's layout is unchanged. New story `Floorplans/Object Page/Layout/Long Form`.
