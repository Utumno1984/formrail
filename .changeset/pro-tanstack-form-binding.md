New entry `@formrail/pro/tanstack-form` (#508): `useTanStackFormBinding(form)` binds a TanStack Form form (`useForm()` of `@tanstack/react-form`) to the `RebaseFormBinding` contract, so `useChangeSummary` and `useFormRebase` of `@formrail/pro/engine` work on it without `@formrail/core` and without `react-hook-form`; `Values` is inferred from `useForm({ defaultValues })`. `binding.values` and `binding.baseline` are immutable snapshots with the same identity across renders that change no value (an inline `defaultValues` included); the baseline follows TanStack Form's live defaults (a new `defaultValues`, also on a touched form; `form.reset(data)`) and is the object passed to `binding.reset(saved)`. What `binding.reset` / `binding.setValues` change (a rebase, `acceptTheirs` / `keepMine`, `reset(saved)`) is written in one update of the form's base store: no field meta, no `listeners.onChange`, no validator, a top-level key removed too, the array version of a `mode="array"` field bumped. The binding never writes TanStack Form's own defaults (`useForm` re-applies its options at every render): the README documents what that means for `isDefaultValue`, `isDirty` and `form.reset()`, and recommends keeping `defaultValues` on the binding's baseline (a `saved` state that follows `binding.baseline`, as the Storybook Example does); after a change of content of `defaultValues` the binding has TanStack Form recompute its field meta, so `isDefaultValue` agrees with the summary at once (on a touched form TanStack Form's `update()` does not recompute it). `isSaving` / `saveError` map `form.handleSubmit()` (`isSubmitting`, a submit that did not succeed), or come from the options for a save of your own. `@tanstack/react-form` (`~1.33.5`, the tested minor only: the adapter reads internals of TanStack Form that are not public API) is an **optional** peer dependency, used for the types only; the main entry, `@formrail/pro/engine` and `@formrail/pro/react-hook-form` do not name it. The package stays private.
