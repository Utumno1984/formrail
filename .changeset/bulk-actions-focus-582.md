`VirtualListReport`: follow-ups of the focus rule of the selection bar (#582). A bulk action that closes its `Tooltip` by force (a `TruncatedText` whose text fits again, which dispatches a `focusout` from code without moving the focus) keeps its tracking, so the focus is still handed to the header checkbox if that action is hidden afterwards; the focus is read in the root of the bar (its document, an iframe's included, or its shadow root). A `focusout` dispatched by the browser still ends the tracking of a shown action, even when the action stays the active element of its document (another window taking the focus, or, in Firefox, the focus moved from a frame to its parent page): hiding that action afterwards never moves the focus back into its document. A control that is not shown when its tracking starts (hidden as it takes the focus, or an environment without layout) no longer leaves its observers running at rest. A focus that lands outside the bar while nothing of the bar is tracked no longer re-renders the component (zero commits while tabbing or typing through other fields). The TSDoc now states the remaining limits: a slot moved to another root is not followed, and `disabled`/`inert` on the focused control are not handled. No public API change.
