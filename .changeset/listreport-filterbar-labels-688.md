`ListReport` forwards four new optional props to its `ListReportFilterBar`: `clearSearchLabel` (name of the button that clears the search), `searchPlaceholder`, `filterBarLabel` (name of the `search` landmark, as `aria-label`) and `filterBarLabelledBy` (as `aria-labelledby`). Without them the output is unchanged.
