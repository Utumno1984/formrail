Data we collect The waitlist form asks for these fields: your email address (required); the size of your team (required); when you would need Formrail Pro (required); the form libraries you use today (optional, you can pick several); your consent to be contacted about Formrail Pro early access and to have your answers used to plan the product, a box you check yourself (required). Without the required fields the form cannot be submitted, so we cannot add you to the waitlist or contact you (art. 13(2)(e) GDPR). Purpose and legal basis Your data is used for two purposes only: to contact you, at the email address you gave, about Formrail Pro early access; to use your answers (team size, form libraries, timing) to plan the product. Both rest on your consent (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by checking a box that is not checked by default. The text of that box is: "I agree that Formrail may use my email address to contact me about Formrail Pro early access, and my answers to plan the product, as described in the privacy notice above." You can withdraw your consent at any time, and withdrawing does not affect what was done before. Where the data goes The form is hosted by Tally, which stores the responses on servers in the European Union and acts as our processor. The responses stay on Tally: they are not copied to email or to any other service, and we read them only there. The form opens from a link; this site embeds nothing from it. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose provider may keep technical server logs under its own policy. We do not sell or share your answers with anyone else. Transfers outside the EEA This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). GitHub processes technical browsing data such as your IP address as an independent controller, under its own privacy statement . The transfer to the United States rests on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. For the form, Tally (Tally BV, Belgium) stores the responses in the European Union. Some of its service providers are located outside the EEA; the list and the safeguards are described on Tally's GDPR page and in its privacy policy . Retention Your responses are kept until you withdraw your consent. The data controller deletes them by hand on Tally no later than 24 months after sign-up, or earlier if you withdraw your consent.
