Data we collect The early access request form asks for these fields: your work email address (required); your company or team (required); the number of developers who would need a license (required); what you would use Formrail Pro for, a description of the type of application, the industry and the problem you want to solve (required); the size of your team (required); the form libraries you use today (optional, you can pick several); when you would need Formrail Pro (required); your consent, a box you check yourself (required). Without the required fields the form cannot be submitted, so we cannot reply to your request or contact you (art. 13(2)(e) GDPR). The form collects no payment data and does not lead to a purchase: nothing is sold through it and no payment is taken. Requests sent before 27 November 2026 contain only the fields of that time: email address, team size, timing, form libraries and consent. The text of the consent box was then: "I agree that Formrail may use my email address to contact me about Formrail Pro early access, and my answers to plan the product, as described in the privacy notice above." For those requests the purposes of that time apply: to contact you about Formrail Pro early access and to use your answers to plan the product. Purpose and legal basis Your data is used for these purposes only: to reply to your early access request; to contact you, at the email address you gave, about Formrail Pro early access; to use your answers (company or team, number of developers, use case, team size, form libraries, timing) to plan the product. All rest on your consent (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by checking a box that is not checked by default. The text of that box is: "I agree that Formrail may use my email address and my answers to reply to my early access request, to contact me about Formrail Pro early access and to plan the product, as described in the privacy notice." You can withdraw your consent at any time, and withdrawing does not affect what was done before. Where the data goes The form is hosted by Tally, which stores the responses on servers in the European Union and acts as our processor. Tally also sends a copy of every request by email to formrail.dev@gmail.com, a Gmail mailbox provided by Google. We read the requests there and on Tally, and nowhere else. The form opens from a link; this site embeds nothing from it. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose provider may keep technical server logs under its own policy. We do not sell or share your answers with anyone else. Google provides the mailbox under its own terms and privacy policy. Google LLC (USA) may process the emails in the United States; the transfer rests on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, to which Google LLC adheres. Transfers outside the EEA This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). GitHub processes technical browsing data such as your IP address as an independent controller, under its own privacy statement . The transfer to the United States rests on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. For the form, Tally (Tally BV, Belgium) stores the responses in the European Union. Some of its service providers are located outside the EEA; the list and the safeguards are described on Tally's GDPR page and in its privacy policy . Retention Your responses are kept until you withdraw your consent. The data controller deletes them by hand, both on Tally and from the mailbox, no later than 24 months after you send the request, or earlier if you withdraw your consent.
