Purchases This section covers the purchase of a Formrail Pro license. The sections above describe the early access request form only, and they do not change. The sale is made by Paddle, as described below; the terms of the license are on the license terms page and the refund rules on the refund policy page. Data we receive We receive from Paddle the data of your purchase that Paddle makes available to us as the licensor of the product. Depending on what you give to Paddle at checkout, it can include: your name; your email address; your company name, if any; your country; your postal code or address and, for a business, the tax or VAT number, when you give them to Paddle at checkout; the number of developers the license is for; the identifier, the date and the amount of the transaction. We also receive the GitHub user names that you give us, for the developers who need access to the private registry. We do not receive your payment card or your bank details: the payment is handled by Paddle. Without your email address and the GitHub user names we cannot grant the access or manage the license; the other data are optional or depend on what Paddle collects at checkout (art. 13(2)(e) GDPR). Purpose and legal basis The data is used for these purposes only: to deliver and manage the license: to grant, check and end the access to the registry, and to answer your requests, including refund requests; to keep the records of the sale for a period that covers the tax and accounting checks that can concern the sale, and to defend ourselves in case of a dispute. The first purpose rests on the contract of sale, art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. The second rests on our legitimate interest in defending ourselves in case of checks or disputes, art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Neither rests on consent, so there is no consent to withdraw for the purchase; the rights listed below still apply, including the right to object to the processing based on legitimate interest (art. 21 GDPR). Paddle, GitHub and the mailbox Paddle (Paddle.com Market Limited, based in the United Kingdom; if you buy from the United States, Paddle.com Inc.; if you buy from Canada, Paddle.com (Canada) Ltd) is the reseller of Formrail Pro: it sells the license to you, takes the payment and issues the invoice. Your receipt names the company that sold to you. For the payment and for its own legal obligations as Merchant of Record, Paddle is an independent controller of your data, under Paddle's privacy notice . The registry is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Packages). The GitHub user names you give us are used to grant access to it, and GitHub processes them, with the technical data of your use of the registry, as an independent controller under GitHub's privacy terms . When you write to us, for instance to ask for a refund, your email arrives in formrail.dev@gmail.com, a Gmail mailbox provided by Google under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else. Transfers outside the EEA Paddle.com Market Limited is based in the United Kingdom, which the European Commission recognises, with an adequacy decision, as giving an adequate level of protection to personal data. Paddle may transfer data outside the EEA; its other companies, and the safeguards it uses, are described in the privacy notice linked above. If you buy from the United States or from Canada, the seller is the local Paddle company (Paddle.com Inc. or Paddle.com (Canada) Ltd), and its transfers are described in that same notice. GitHub Inc. and Google LLC are based in the United States: the transfer of data to them rests on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, to which both adhere. We do not transfer your purchase data anywhere else. Retention We keep the records of the sale for 7 years from the end of the year in which the sale takes place, to defend ourselves in case of checks or disputes: this is a period that covers the tax and accounting checks that can concern the sale. Of the purchase data received from Paddle, the records of the sale keep only the identifier of the transaction, the date, the amount, your country and your email address; the emails about a purchase, described below, are records of the sale too. After that the data controller deletes them. Until then you can object to this keeping (art. 21 GDPR), as described in "Your rights" below. The other purchase data received from Paddle, such as your name, your company name, your address and your tax or VAT number, are not records of the sale, and they are not kept for that period. The data controller deletes them at the end of the 12 months of updates of your license or, if you buy again, of the last license you buy, or earlier if the license ends: when you receive a refund or the payment is reversed, or for a serious violation, as described in the license terms. The GitHub user names are not records of the sale, and they are not kept for that period. The data controller deletes them when your access to the registry ends: when the 12 months of updates end and you do not buy again, when you receive a refund or the payment is reversed, or when the license ends for a serious violation, as described in the license terms. When you replace a developer, the name of the replaced developer is deleted when the access moves to the replacement. The emails you send to formrail.dev@gmail.com about a purchase (a refund, the replacement of a developer, a problem with the license) are records of the sale: they are kept for the same period, 7 years from the end of the year in which the sale takes place. The other emails follow the rule in "Emails you send us" below.
