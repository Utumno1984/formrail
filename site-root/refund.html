Refund policy

You can ask for a refund of Formrail Pro within {{REFUND_DAYS}} days of the purchase, without giving a reason, whoever you are, except in case of fraud or abuse. Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who can ask, and until when

You can ask for a refund within {{REFUND_DAYS}} days of the purchase, without giving a reason. This is true for everyone who buys Formrail Pro, companies and consumers alike, except in case of fraud or abuse. A request made on day {{REFUND_DAYS}} after the purchase is still within the term.

How to ask

There are two ways, and you can use either one:

  • through Paddle, the seller of your purchase: open Paddle's buyer support page and use the details of your receipt;
  • by email to {{LICENSOR_EMAIL}}: write the email address you used to buy, your company name, if any, and the transaction identifier of your receipt if you have it. You do not have to say why.

Paddle is the Merchant of Record: it sells to you, collects the payment and the taxes and issues the invoice, so the money is returned by Paddle, to the payment method you used. The time the money takes to arrive depends on Paddle and on your payment provider.

What happens to your access

A refund ends the license and the access to the private registry on GitHub Packages. You must stop using Formrail Pro and delete the copies you downloaded. The same happens if the payment is reversed (a chargeback): it is treated as a refund. The license text says it in the section Refund and termination.

If you were refunded, you can still use Formrail Core, which is free under the MIT license and is not affected.

Consumers in the EU and the UK

This policy gives you more than the law requires of a seller, and it does not take away any right you have as a consumer. The rights of consumers in the European Union and the United Kingdom, including the right to withdraw from the purchase, are set out in Paddle's Buyer Terms. Starting to use Formrail Pro does not shorten the {{REFUND_DAYS}} days of this policy; your rights under Paddle's Buyer Terms apply in addition.
