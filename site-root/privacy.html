Privacy notice

Information under art. 13 of the GDPR for people who join the Formrail Pro waitlist. It is plain information, not a contract.

Data controller

Formrail is a project by Daniele Ingusci, who is the data controller. Contact for privacy requests: {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}.

Data we collect

The waitlist form asks for these fields:

  • your email address (required);
  • the size of your team (required);
  • when you would need Formrail Pro (required);
  • the form libraries you use today (optional, you can pick several);
  • your consent to be contacted about Formrail Pro early access and to have your answers used to plan the product, a box you check yourself (required).

Without the required fields the form cannot be submitted, so we cannot add you to the waitlist or contact you (art. 13(2)(e) GDPR).

Purpose and legal basis

Your data is used for two purposes only:

  • to contact you, at the email address you gave, about Formrail Pro early access;
  • to use your answers (team size, form libraries, timing) to plan the product.

Both rest on your consent (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by checking a box that is not checked by default. The text of that box is: "I agree that Formrail may use my email address to contact me about Formrail Pro early access, and my answers to plan the product, as described in the privacy notice above." You can withdraw your consent at any time, and withdrawing does not affect what was done before.

Where the data goes

The form is hosted by Tally, which stores the responses on servers in the European Union and acts as our processor. The responses stay on Tally: they are not copied to email or to any other service, and we read them only there. The form opens from a link; this site embeds nothing from it. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose provider may keep technical server logs under its own policy. We do not sell or share your answers with anyone else.

Retention

Your responses are kept until you withdraw your consent. The data controller deletes them by hand on Tally no later than 24 months after sign-up, or earlier if you withdraw your consent.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and a portable copy, and you can withdraw consent, by writing to the address above. You can also complain to your supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). There is no automated decision-making.

No cookies, no analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. It loads no fonts, scripts or content from other sites.
