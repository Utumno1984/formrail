Privacy notice

Information under art. 13 of the GDPR for people who join the Formrail Pro waitlist. It is plain information, not a contract.

Data controller

Daniele Ingusci. Contact for privacy requests: {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}.

Data we collect

The waitlist form asks for these fields:

  • your email address (required);
  • the size of your team (required);
  • when you would need Formrail Pro (required);
  • the form libraries you use today (optional, you can pick several);
  • your consent to be contacted about Formrail Pro, a box you check yourself (required).

Without the required fields the form cannot be submitted, so we cannot add you to the waitlist or contact you (art. 13(2)(e) GDPR).

Purpose and legal basis

Purpose: to contact you about Formrail Pro (availability, license terms, launch) and to understand the demand for it. Legal basis: your consent (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by checking a box that is not checked by default. You can withdraw it at any time, and withdrawing does not affect what was done before.

Where the data goes

The form is hosted by Tally, which stores the responses on servers in the European Union and acts as our processor. The form opens from a link; this site embeds nothing from it. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose provider may keep technical server logs under its own policy. We do not sell or share your answers with anyone else.

Retention

Until you withdraw your consent or the waitlist is closed, and in any case no longer than 12 months after the Pro launch.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and a portable copy, and you can withdraw consent, by writing to the address above. You can also complain to your supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). There is no automated decision-making.

No cookies, no analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. It loads no fonts, scripts or content from other sites.
