Privacy notice

Information under art. 13 of the GDPR for people who join the Formrail Pro waitlist, and, in its own section further down, for teams that send a design partner application, which is a different form, and, in its own section, for people who buy a Formrail Pro license; for the purchase data that we receive from Paddle, it is also the information under art. 14 of the GDPR. It is plain information, not a contract.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Data controller

Formrail is a project by Daniele Ingusci, who is the data controller. Contact for privacy requests: {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}.

Data we collect

The waitlist form asks for these fields:

  • your email address (required);
  • the size of your team (required);
  • when you would need Formrail Pro (required);
  • the form libraries you use today (optional, you can pick several);
  • your consent to be contacted about Formrail Pro early access and to have your answers used to plan the product, a box you check yourself (required).

Without the required fields the form cannot be submitted, so we cannot add you to the waitlist or contact you (art. 13(2)(e) GDPR).

Purpose and legal basis

Your data is used for two purposes only:

  • to contact you, at the email address you gave, about Formrail Pro early access;
  • to use your answers (team size, form libraries, timing) to plan the product.

Both rest on your consent (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by checking a box that is not checked by default. The text of that box is: "I agree that Formrail may use my email address to contact me about Formrail Pro early access, and my answers to plan the product, as described in the privacy notice above." You can withdraw your consent at any time, and withdrawing does not affect what was done before.

Where the data goes

The form is hosted by Tally, which stores the responses on servers in the European Union and acts as our processor. The responses stay on Tally: they are not copied to email or to any other service, and we read them only there. The form opens from a link; this site embeds nothing from it. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose provider may keep technical server logs under its own policy. We do not sell or share your answers with anyone else.

Transfers outside the EEA

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). GitHub processes technical browsing data such as your IP address as an independent controller, under its own privacy statement. The transfer to the United States rests on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

For the form, Tally (Tally BV, Belgium) stores the responses in the European Union. Some of its service providers are located outside the EEA; the list and the safeguards are described on Tally's GDPR page and in its privacy policy.

Retention

Your responses are kept until you withdraw your consent. The data controller deletes them by hand on Tally no later than 24 months after sign-up, or earlier if you withdraw your consent.

Design partner applications

This section covers the design partner application form of the Formrail Pro page for teams. It is a different form from the waitlist one, with its own fields, and the sections above describe the waitlist form only.

Data we collect

The design partner application form asks for these fields, all required:

  • Work email;
  • Your role;
  • What are you building? (a description of the type of application and the industry);
  • Team size (one of 1–3, 4–10, 11–50 or 50+);
  • Form library in use (you can pick several from a fixed list, which includes Custom, None and Other);
  • When would you need it? (one of Now, 1–3 months, 3–6 months or Later);
  • Are you available for two short feedback calls? (Yes or No);
  • May we mention your company publicly as a design partner? (Yes or No);
  • Consent, a box you check yourself that refers to this notice.

Without the required fields the form cannot be submitted, so we cannot evaluate your application or contact your team (art. 13(2)(e) GDPR).

Purpose and legal basis

Your data is used for two purposes:

  • to evaluate the application of your team;
  • to contact your team, at the work email you gave, about the design partner programme.

Both rest on your consent (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by checking a box that is not checked by default. You can withdraw it at any time, and withdrawing does not affect what was done before. Your answer to the question about public mentions only records whether we may name your company as a design partner; we do not name a company that answered No.

Where the data goes and retention

Like the waitlist form, this form is hosted by Tally, which stores the responses on servers in the European Union and acts as our processor; the transfers outside the EEA described above apply to it as well. Unlike the waitlist form, Tally also sends a copy of every application by email to formrail.dev@gmail.com, a Gmail mailbox provided by Google. We read the applications there and on Tally, and nowhere else. The form opens from a link; this site embeds nothing from it.

Google provides the mailbox under its own terms and privacy policy. Google LLC (USA) may process the emails in the United States; the transfer rests on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, to which Google LLC adheres.

Responses are kept until you withdraw your consent. The data controller deletes them by hand, both on Tally and from the mailbox, no later than 24 months after your application, or earlier if you withdraw your consent. The rights listed below apply to this form too.

Purchases

This section covers the purchase of a Formrail Pro license. The sections above describe the waitlist form only, and they do not change. The sale is made by Paddle, as described below; the terms of the license are on the license terms page and the refund rules on the refund policy page.

Data we receive

We receive from Paddle the data of your purchase that Paddle makes available to us as the seller of the product. Depending on what you give to Paddle at checkout, it can include:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • your company name, if any;
  • your country;
  • your postal code or address and, for a business, the tax or VAT number, when you give them to Paddle at checkout;
  • the number of developers the license is for;
  • the identifier of the transaction.

We also receive the GitHub user names that you give us, for the developers who need access to the private registry. We do not receive your payment card or your bank details: the payment is handled by Paddle. Without these data we cannot grant the access or manage the license (art. 13(2)(e) GDPR).

Purpose and legal basis

The data is used for these purposes only:

  • to deliver and manage the license: to grant, check and end the access to the registry, and to answer your requests, including refund requests;
  • to comply with the legal obligations that apply to a sale, for instance the keeping of accounting and tax records.

The first purpose rests on the contract of sale, art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. The second rests on a legal obligation, art. 6(1)(c) GDPR. Neither rests on consent, so there is no consent to withdraw for the purchase; the rights listed below still apply.

Paddle, GitHub and the mailbox

Paddle (Paddle.com Market Limited, based in the United Kingdom; if you buy from the United States, Paddle.com Inc.; if you buy from Canada, Paddle.com (Canada) Ltd. Your receipt names the company that sold to you) is the reseller of Formrail Pro: it sells the license to you, takes the payment and issues the invoice. For the payment and for its own legal obligations as Merchant of Record, Paddle is an independent controller of your data, under Paddle's privacy notice.

The registry is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Packages). The GitHub user names you give us are used to grant access to it, and GitHub processes them, with the technical data of your use of the registry, as an independent controller under GitHub's privacy terms.

When you write to us, for instance to ask for a refund, your email arrives in formrail.dev@gmail.com, a Gmail mailbox provided by Google under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Transfers outside the EEA

Paddle.com Market Limited is based in the United Kingdom, which the European Commission recognises, with an adequacy decision, as giving an adequate level of protection to personal data. Paddle may transfer data outside the EEA; its other companies, and the safeguards it uses, are described in the privacy notice linked above. If you buy from the United States or from Canada, the seller is the local Paddle company (Paddle.com Inc. or Paddle.com (Canada) Ltd), and its transfers are described in that same notice. GitHub Inc. and Google LLC are based in the United States: the transfer of data to them rests on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, to which both adhere. We do not transfer your purchase data anywhere else.

Retention

We keep the purchase data received from Paddle for {{RETENTION_YEARS}} years from the end of the year in which the sale takes place: this is the period for which tax and accounting law requires the records of the sale to be kept. After that the data controller deletes it. Where the law requires a record to be kept, you can ask for its erasure only after that period.

The GitHub user names are not records of the sale, and they are not kept for that period. The data controller deletes them when your access to the registry ends: when the 12 months of updates end and you do not buy again, when you receive a refund or the payment is reversed, or when the license ends for a serious violation, as described in the license terms.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and a portable copy, and you can withdraw consent, by writing to the address above. You can also complain to your supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). There is no automated decision-making.

No cookies, no analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. It loads no fonts, scripts or content from other sites.

Every page of this site keeps this promise. If a page ever needs to load a script or content from another site, this notice will say so before that page is published.
